What to do before help arrives

Safety comes before cleanup. These steps are for water you can reach without standing in it.

1Find the source

Shut the fixture valve or the main valve if it is dry and within reach.

2Protect people

Keep children and pets away from deep water and from anything plugged in nearby.

3Check ceilings

A bulging or dripping ceiling should be avoided until a crew has checked it.

4Document

Photograph the stain, the wet floor and the source before moving anything.

5Move what is dry

Lift furniture legs and carry papers and electronics to a dry room.

6Call dispatch

Describe the source and ask what to do until a crew can look.

What shapes the cost in Cimarron Woods

Scope and price are explained before work starts, and they follow what the water touched.

  • Hidden moisture. Water inside a wall or ceiling cavity needs readings to find its edge.
  • Finish level. Upgraded flooring, trim and cabinets change the repair path.
  • Layout. A two-story house and a walkout ranch route water differently.
  • Drying time. Dry-out days follow readings, not a fixed schedule.

Insurance treatment depends on the cause of the loss and your policy. Ask your insurer, and keep photos, readings and the written scope together.
