A sewage backup is an emergency and a contamination event, and La Vista dispatch is open 24/7. Stay out of the affected area, keep children and pets away, stop using sinks, toilets and the washer so no more water enters the drain, and keep power off in wet rooms unless the panel is dry. Do not mix cleaning chemicals.

Sewage backup cleanup is the removal and sanitizing of Category 3 water, the most contaminated class under ANSI/IICRC S500. It is not the same as clean water cleanup. Porous materials touched by sewage are usually removed and surfaces are cleaned and treated before drying.

Sewage smell or a floor drain backing up: what is happening?

A sewage smell from drains, gurgling fixtures or water rising in a floor drain usually means a blockage in the line or the main. If only one fixture is affected, the blockage is often in your lateral. If several fixtures back up at once, it may be farther down the line.

The City of La Vista Public Works Department, which handles sewer, takes calls at 402-331-8927 during business hours and has an after-hours emergency line at 402-593-4111. Call them if you suspect a main line problem.

Sewage backup: what to do first

Contamination decides everything. Protect people, then the building.

1Stay out

Do not walk through sewage and do not let children or pets near it.

2Stop water use

Do not flush, run the washer or use sinks while the line is blocked.

3Do not use a household vacuum

A standard vacuum spreads contamination. Leave extraction to the crew.

4Skip the bleach mix

Never combine bleach with ammonia or other cleaners. Open windows if odors are strong.

5Document it

Photograph the affected area from a safe distance for your claim.

6Call Public Works if needed

If neighbors are affected too, call the city's line before or alongside ours.

Why sewage water is handled differently

Sewage can carry bacteria, viruses and parasites. That is why porous items such as carpet, pad, insulation, upholstery and drywall are normally removed instead of cleaned, and why workers use protective gear.

Questions about health effects belong to a physician. For the building, the job is to remove contaminated material, clean and sanitize hard surfaces, and dry the structure.

How the cleanup works

Affected finishes are rebuilt after drying. Standing water from outside belongs under flood damage cleanup, and the drying stage mirrors structural drying.

1

Containment

The crew limits the spread of contaminated water and air.

2

Removal

Water and contaminated porous materials are removed.

3

Clean and sanitize

Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated.

4

Dry and verify

Dehumidifiers and air movers run to a dry reference, then the space is checked.

What shapes the cost of sewage cleanup

Sewage cleanup is scoped carefully because contaminated materials must be removed. A written scope and price are explained before work starts.

  • Area affected. More square footage means more removal and sanitizing.
  • Materials removed. Carpet, pad, drywall and insulation are removed and rebuilt.
  • Contamination duration. Longer exposure leaves more to remove.
  • Protective measures. Containment and protective gear add steps.
  • Drying. Deep cleaning is followed by drying equipment.

The Nebraska Department of Insurance says water backup coverage is a separate endorsement. Check your policy before assuming a sewer backup is covered. Wet carpet and drywall that touched contaminated water are covered under carpet water damage restoration and wall and drywall repair.

Sewage backup questions in La Vista

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in La Vista?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7. Stay out of the affected area and call, and we will schedule same-day response when a crew is available.

Who do I call first, the city or you?

If several homes are affected, call the City of La Vista Public Works at 402-331-8927 or the after-hours line at 402-593-4111. If the backup is in your own line, call a plumber and us.

Can I clean up sewage myself?

Small spills on hard surfaces can be handled with protective gear, but anything that soaked carpet, drywall or insulation needs trained crews.

Is a sewage backup covered by homeowners insurance?

Often only with a water backup endorsement. The state insurance department recommends asking your agent.

How long before I can use the room again?

After removal, sanitizing and drying. The crew confirms readings and finishes are safe before rebuild begins.
