Service Area: La Vista, Springfield and Sarpy County

Where our crews work, and how to find out whether your address is covered.

☎ Call (531) 202-9921

Our dispatch line is open 24/7, and our base is in La Vista, ZIP 68128. From there crews work across the east side of Sarpy County and into nearby parts of the Omaha metro. The map below shows the region we think of as our coverage area.

La Vista, NE 68128

La Vista is where we are based. The city sits between Omaha and Ralston to the north, Papillion to the south and Bellevue to the east, with Interstate 80 on its west side. For a loss at home, start with the La Vista water damage restoration page or call the line.

Springfield, NE 68059

Springfield is a small Sarpy County town southwest of La Vista, with its own creek, its own history and its own city office. We have a dedicated page at water damage restoration in Springfield, NE that covers local flooding and runoff.

La Vista neighborhoods

Five La Vista neighborhoods have their own pages: Southwind, Val Verde, Cimarron Woods, Park View Heights and Mayfair. Each page covers only housing and drainage details we could confirm, and says plainly where we could not confirm a flood zone.

Other communities we take calls from

We also take calls from Papillion and Bellevue, and from Gretna (68028), Boys Town (68010), Elkhorn and the area around Offutt Air Force Base. In Omaha we work ZIPs such as 68101 through 68104. Bellevue ZIPs include 68005, 68123 and 68147. Plattsmouth is a longer drive, so call before assuming either way.

Not sure whether you are in range? Call the dispatch line, give your street address and ask. If we cannot reach you quickly, we will say so rather than leave you waiting.

What every call covers

Every call starts the same way, whether you live in La Vista or in Springfield. We ask about the source, the depth and the materials. Then we explain the plan and the price before work starts. See the full list of water damage services to find the page that matches your situation.
