Kitchen water damage is the water that reaches cabinets, countertops, flooring and walls from a sink, dishwasher, ice-maker line or supply fitting. Kitchens hold more water-using fixtures in a small space than any other room, and cabinet bases hide leaks until they are well advanced.

If you see water, close the shutoff valves under the sink or the main, move everything out of the cabinet and keep power off at any outlet that is wet. Our dispatch line is open 24/7 for La Vista.

Water damage under the kitchen sink

A slow drip from a drain trap, a loose supply connection or a cracked disposer housing can run for weeks. The first signs are a swollen cabinet floor, a musty smell, peeling finish on the cabinet interior and a soft spot in the flooring near the sink.

Because the water collects in the cabinet base, it soaks the particleboard and moves into the subfloor and toe kick. Checking only the visible puddle misses most of it.

The crew uses a moisture meter on the cabinet floor, toe kick and wall behind it. Small cabinet repairs often make sense, while particleboard that has swollen is usually replaced.

What the restoration involves

If a pipe failure above caused the leak, see burst pipe water damage. If the source is a machine, appliance leak water damage covers it.

1

Stop and extract

Close the shutoff and remove standing water from the cabinet and floor.

2

Open and inspect

Toe kicks come off and baseboards are lifted where readings are wet.

3

Dry in place

Air movers and small dehumidifiers dry cabinet bases and subfloor.

4

Repair

Damaged cabinet parts, flooring and drywall are replaced after readings are dry.

Plumbing, permits and who does what

The restoration crew dries and repairs materials. A plumber repairs the leaking trap, valve or supply line. The City of La Vista Building Department handles plumbing permits, including replacement or removal of plumbing fixtures, so ask at 402-593-6400 when a repair goes beyond a simple washer.

Water that was clean at the source, such as a supply line from the Metropolitan Utilities District, starts as Category 1. That is the best case for drying in place, and it changes if the water sits for days.

Dishwashers, ice makers and refrigerator lines

Dishwasher supply hoses, door gaskets and ice-maker lines are common sources of slow kitchen leaks. The water often travels along the cabinet run and into the flooring beside the appliance, so the damage shows up a few feet from the real source.

Pulling the appliance forward and checking the floor beneath it is part of an honest inspection. Tile or vinyl over plywood can hide a wet subfloor for a long time, because the surface does not show moisture. A meter reading through the flooring, plus a check at the nearest toe kick, gives a better answer than looking at the surface.

What shapes the cost of kitchen water damage

Kitchen repairs vary with cabinet material and flooring. A written scope and price are explained before work starts.

  • Leak duration. A leak caught in a day is a drying job, while a weeks-long leak may mean replacement.
  • Cabinet material. Particleboard is replaced more often than plywood or solid wood.
  • Flooring. Vinyl, laminate, tile and hardwood each react differently.
  • Countertops and plumbing. Removing and resetting countertops adds steps.
  • Drying time. Wall and floor cavities behind cabinets dry slowly.

Sudden kitchen leaks are often covered, and slow leaks often are not. Ask your insurer, and keep the failed part and photos. If the leak soaked the floor beyond the cabinets, the hardwood floor water damage repair page explains how wood and laminate are handled. A newer La Vista subdivision example is covered on the Cimarron Woods page.

Drying cabinet bases and subfloors follows ANSI/IICRC S500 practice, with readings taken at the toe kick and the wall behind it. Before help arrives, close the shutoffs and do not use the dishwasher or disposer until the area has been checked.

Kitchen water damage questions in La Vista

Can kitchen cabinets be saved after a leak?

Often yes if they are solid wood or plywood and dried quickly. Swollen particleboard is usually replaced.

How do I know the subfloor is wet?

A soft spot, a musty smell or a moisture meter reading above dry reference. Drying the surface is not enough.

Do you replace countertops?

We handle drying and the restoration scope. Countertop removal and reset may be part of it, depending on the cabinet repair.

Do I need a plumber too?

Yes, for the leaking fitting. The restoration crew dries and repairs the damage.

Do you take kitchen leak calls at night?

The dispatch line is open 24/7. If water is still running, call right away.
